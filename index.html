The Science

Built on peer-reviewed research.

LiftIQ's programming logic is aligned with the 2026 ACSM Position Stand on Resistance Training — an umbrella review synthesizing 137 systematic reviews and data from more than 30,000 participants.

Progressive overload

Load and volume are tracked per-lift. The app surfaces small, manageable jumps over time — the single strongest driver of strength and hypertrophy adaptation.

Frequency matters

Programs target each major movement pattern at least twice per week, matching the training frequencies that consistently produce adaptation in the literature.

Full range of motion

The exercise library emphasises working through a full ROM, with form cues for each lift. Full-ROM training is associated with stronger hypertrophy outcomes than partials.

Individualization over rigid periodization

Your goal, experience, and recovery drive programming decisions. The research shows structured progression matters more than sticking to a specific periodization model.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Still have questions? Reach out at support@liftiqsolutions.app.

How is LiftIQ different from other gym apps?

LiftIQ is the only AI-powered strength training app built on the 2026 ACSM Position Stand on Resistance Training. Unlike generic trackers, it suggests the exact weight you should use on your next set based on your recent RPE, rep performance, and recovery.

It is also fully bilingual (English and Spanish), runs on both iPhone and iPad, and has a strict no-ads and no-data-selling policy.

Do I need to pay to use LiftIQ?

LiftIQ starts with a 14-day free trial of Pro. After that, Pro is $9.99 per month or $59.99 per year.

You can cancel anytime directly from your App Store subscriptions — no emails, no friction.

Does LiftIQ work without internet?

Yes — you can log workouts and view your history offline. The app syncs automatically once you reconnect.

Features that require a connection: the AI Coach chat, photo-based nutrition estimation, and barcode lookups.

What iPhone or iPad do I need?

LiftIQ supports iOS and iPadOS 15.1 and newer. It is a universal app, so a single purchase works on both iPhone and iPad with a shared account.

How accurate is the AI photo nutrition estimation?

Photo-based macro estimates are convenient for home-cooked meals but not laboratory-precise — they typically land within 15–25% of true values.

For packaged foods, use the barcode scanner, which reads exact macros from the product database. Both methods let you edit any value before saving.

Do I need an Apple Watch to use LiftIQ?

No. Apple Watch is fully optional. LiftIQ integrates with Apple Health to read steps and active calories, and to write back workouts and body weight — but every core feature works on iPhone alone.

What happens to my data if I cancel or delete my account?

Your data is encrypted at rest and you can export it at any time.

If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. LiftIQ does not sell your data or share it with advertisers — ever.

Is LiftIQ safe for beginners?

The programming adapts to your stated experience level, and the exercise library includes form cues for every lift.

That said, LiftIQ offers general fitness information for educational purposes only — if you have any health condition, past injury, or concern, please consult a qualified professional before starting any training program.
