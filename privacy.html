Privacy Policy

LiftIQ is built on the principle that your fitness data belongs to you. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control or delete it.

Last updated: March 1, 2026

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth, never in plain text). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only a unique identifier and, optionally, your name — no passwords.

Profile Information

Information you voluntarily provide: display name, profile photo, date of birth, biological sex, height, weight, fitness goals, activity level, dietary preferences, and training preferences. All of this is used to personalise your experience.

Fitness & Health Data

  • Workout sessions, sets, reps, and weights you log in the App
  • Body weight measurements you submit
  • Daily step counts (read from your device's motion sensors)
  • Nutrition logs including meals, calories, and macronutrients
  • With your explicit permission: we read Active Energy Burned and Step Count from Apple Health, and write completed workouts and body weight back to Apple Health

Food Photos

Food photos you submit for nutritional analysis are sent to our AI processing partner (Groq, Inc.) for image recognition. Photos are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored on our servers. Processing is subject to Groq's data handling policies.

AI Coach Messages

Free-text messages you send to the AI Coach are forwarded to Groq, Inc. for processing. Messages you send to the AI Coach are processed by Groq, Inc. to generate responses. We send your recent conversation history (up to 20 messages) for context. We store a daily message count server-side to enforce usage limits.

⚠️ Avoid sharing sensitive medical or financial information in AI Coach messages.

Usage & Analytics

Anonymised event data (e.g. "workout completed", "meal logged") to understand how the App is used and to improve it. No personally identifiable information is attached to analytics events.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App and its features
  • To personalise your training plans, macro targets, and coaching experience
  • To sync data with Apple Health (iOS only, with your permission)
  • To process subscription payments via RevenueCat and Apple / Google
  • To enforce fair-use limits on AI features
  • To investigate and fix bugs and crashes (via Sentry)
  • To communicate with you about your account or important updates
🔒 We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Period.

AI Coaching Services

LiftIQ's AI Coach feature uses artificial intelligence to provide personalized fitness guidance. When you use the AI Coach, your messages and relevant profile data (such as fitness goals, training schedule, and health conditions you have disclosed) are sent to a third-party AI service provider (Groq, Inc.) to generate responses. This data is used solely to produce your coaching responses and is not stored by the AI provider beyond the duration of the request.

The AI Coach is powered by Meta's Llama large language model, operated through Groq's infrastructure. By using the AI Coach feature, you consent to your coaching messages being processed by this third-party service. You can stop using the AI Coach at any time by navigating away from the chat.

We do not sell your data to AI providers or use your data to train AI models.

Website Analytics

Our public marketing website (liftiqsolutions.app) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate visitor traffic. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookie-free and privacy-preserving by design: it does not set any cookies, does not use fingerprinting, and does not collect personally identifiable information.

The data collected is limited to aggregate counts such as page views, browser type, and country. We use this information only to understand overall traffic trends to the website. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for full details.

Note: Cloudflare Web Analytics runs only on the public website. It does not run inside the LiftIQ mobile app.

Data Retention

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • Workout & nutrition logs: retained until account deletion
  • AI chat messages: stored in our database, deleted on account deletion
  • Food photos: processed in real-time, not stored permanently
  • Crash reports (Sentry): retained up to 90 days
  • Analytics events: retained until account deletion

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Row-level security on all database tables — each user can only access their own data
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Profile photos are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Account Deletion

When you delete your account (via Profile → Delete Account), the following is permanently removed: your profile, workout history, nutrition logs, body measurements, AI chat history, personal records, and training plans. Crash reports from our error monitoring service (Sentry) may be retained for up to 90 days. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through your device's subscription settings.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data — use the in-app Delete Account feature
  • Export your data — contact us
  • Opt out of analytics — contact us

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you specific rights regarding your personal information.

Categories of Personal Information Collected

  • Identifiers: email address, display name, user ID
  • Health & fitness data: body weight, height, body measurements (chest, waist, arms), biological sex, date of birth, health conditions, activity level
  • Nutrition data: meal logs, food photos, macro targets, dietary preferences
  • Fitness data: workout logs, exercise sets/reps/weights, personal records, training plans, step counts
  • Device data: device model, OS version, crash reports, performance data
  • AI interaction data: chat messages with AI Coach
🔒 We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Rights Under CCPA

  • Right to Know: You may request details about the personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise these rights, email support@liftiqsolutions.app. We will respond within 45 days of receiving your verifiable request.

For Users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provides you with additional rights.

Legal Basis for Processing

Processing Activity Legal Basis
Account creation & app functionality Contract (Article 6(1)(b))
Health data processing Explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a))
Analytics & crash reporting Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f))
Marketing communications Consent (Article 6(1)(a))

Health-related data (body weight, measurements, biological sex, etc.) is classified as Special Category Data under Article 9 of the GDPR and is processed only with your explicit consent.

International Data Transfers

Your data is processed in the United States. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the legal mechanism for transferring personal data from the EEA to the US.

Data Retention

Account data is retained until you request deletion. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.

Your Additional GDPR Rights

  • Right to data portability
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

Data Protection contact: support@liftiqsolutions.app
