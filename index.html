The Science

Built on peer-reviewed research.

LiftIQ's programming logic is aligned with the 2026 ACSM Position Stand on Resistance Training — an umbrella review synthesizing 137 systematic reviews and data from more than 30,000 participants.

Progressive overload

Load and volume are tracked per-lift. The app surfaces small, manageable jumps over time — the single strongest driver of strength and hypertrophy adaptation.

Frequency matters

Programs target each major movement pattern at least twice per week, matching the training frequencies that consistently produce adaptation in the literature.

Full range of motion

The exercise library emphasises working through a full ROM, with form cues for each lift. Full-ROM training is associated with stronger hypertrophy outcomes than partials.

Individualization over rigid periodization

Your goal, experience, and recovery drive programming decisions. The research shows structured progression matters more than sticking to a specific periodization model.
