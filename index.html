FAQ

Before
your first rep.

Still have questions? Reach out at support@liftiqsolutions.app.

How is LiftIQ different from other gym apps?

LiftIQ is an AI-powered strength training app built on the 2026 ACSM Position Stand on Resistance Training. It suggests a weight for your next set based on your recent RPE, rep performance, and recovery.

It is also fully bilingual (English and Spanish), runs on both iPhone and iPad, and has a strict no-ads and no-data-selling policy.

Do I need to pay to use LiftIQ?

Logging workouts and tracking nutrition is free for good: 184 exercises with real demo videos, macro targets, and your meal plan. Pro includes an AI coach and adaptive workout plans, starting with a 7-day free trial, then $9.99 per month or $59.99 per year.

You can cancel anytime in your App Store subscriptions.

Does LiftIQ work without internet?

Yes. You can log workouts and view your history offline. The app syncs automatically once you reconnect.

Features that require a connection: the AI Coach chat, photo-based nutrition estimation, and barcode lookups.

What iPhone or iPad do I need?

LiftIQ supports iOS and iPadOS 15.1 and newer. It is a universal app, so a single purchase works on both iPhone and iPad with a shared account.

How accurate is the AI photo nutrition estimation?

Photo analysis provides an estimate of your meal’s macros. Review the portions and values before saving.

For packaged foods, use the barcode scanner, which reads exact macros from the product database. Both methods let you edit any value before saving.

Do I need an Apple Watch to use LiftIQ?

No. Apple Watch is fully optional. LiftIQ integrates with Apple Health to read steps and active calories, and to write back workouts and body weight. Every core feature works on iPhone alone.

What happens to my data if I cancel or delete my account?

Your data is encrypted at rest and you can export it at any time.

If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. LiftIQ does not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Is LiftIQ safe for beginners?

The programming adapts to your stated experience level, and the exercise library includes form cues for every lift.

LiftIQ offers general fitness information for educational purposes only. If you have any health condition, past injury, or concern, please consult a qualified professional before starting any training program.
